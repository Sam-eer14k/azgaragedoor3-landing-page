# AZ Garage Door: conversion wireframe

Written before any code. It records what each section is for, what the visitor
is thinking when they reach it, and what it has to do to move them one step
closer to picking up the phone.

## The business, and what that means for the page

AZ Garage Door is an owner-operated repair company working the Phoenix valley
since 2011. The work is reactive. A spring snaps, an opener dies, a door comes
off its track, and somebody needs it fixed today. Often with a car shut inside.

Three things follow from that.

The visitor is not browsing. They are in a small emergency, usually on a phone,
sometimes standing in the garage looking at the broken thing. Every screen has
to put a phone number within thumb reach.

The conversion is a call, not a form and not a checkout. The form exists for the
person who would rather type at 11pm than talk to anyone.

The competitive problem is trust, not awareness. Garage door repair has a
reputation for quoting low on the phone and inflating at the door. So the page
is built around one promise: the number we say is the number you pay.

## Reference layout

The Roofsie template supplies the design language and the section order. It is a
roofing template, which sells planned, high-consideration craftsmanship, so its
shells get repointed toward an unplanned, price-sensitive repair call. The order
and the visual rhythm stay.

| Roofsie section | Kept as | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hero slider | Hero slider, same structure | Replicated exactly per the brief. Three slides, eyebrow, big headline, sub, one CTA, arrows. |
| Three feature cards | Three feature cards | Straight-price, licensed-free honesty, free estimate. The three things a caller asks first. |
| About with percent badge | About with 2,400+ badge | A real count from the business beats a made-up satisfaction percentage. |
| Services three-up | Services, six items | Six actual failure types, ordered by how often the phone rings for each. |

## Section order and job

1. **Header.** Top bar carries hours, email and socials. Main bar carries logo,
   nav, the phone number, and a free estimate button. On mobile it collapses to
   a burger and a call button.
2. **Hero.** Three slides on a 5 second fade. Eyebrow names the coverage area,
   the headline names the service, the sub-line sets the promise, one CTA. Copied
   structurally from the sample as briefed.
3. **Feature cards.** Three cards that answer the first three objections before
   they form: the price is flat, the estimate is free, we tell you what we are
   and are not.
4. **About.** Photo, a floating 2,400+ badge, and two proof points. Establishes
   the business is real, local and has been at it since 2011.
5. **Services.** Six repair types with a from-price on each. A visitor who knows
   what broke can self-select and skip ahead.
6. **Why choose us.** Four reasons in a split layout over a dark image. Upfront
   pricing, five-year spring warranty, same rate around the clock, no trip fee.
7. **Recent work.** Carousel of real jobs, labelled by city and work done.
8. **Price band.** Full-bleed dark. Four flat prices stated plainly. The
   strongest differentiator gets the loudest section.
9. **Testimonials.** Two reviews with name, city and job type.
10. **FAQ.** Three questions, each a live objection: same-day, trip fee, why
    both springs get replaced.
11. **CTA strip.** For the visitor who scrolled past everything and still needs
    a nudge.
12. **Footer.** Number, hours, coverage, and the licensing disclosure.
13. **Sticky mobile call bar.** Present on every screen under 768px.

## Hierarchy

The phone number appears in the header, the hero, the price band, the CTA strip,
the footer, and the sticky mobile bar. Nothing else repeats more than twice.

Type scale runs 13 / 15 / 17 / 20 / 28 / 40 / 64. Section headings sit at 40,
the hero at 64, and prices get tabular figures so the columns line up.

## Colour

Taken from the logo, not invented. Royal blue #1A3E9C as the brand field, red
#E01B24 as the action colour, yellow #FFE000 reserved for the AZ mark and
nothing else. Light theme throughout: white surfaces, #F4F6FB section bands,
#0E1421 for text.

Red is the only colour used on buttons and links, so on any given screen the
thing to click is unambiguous.

## Mobile

Order holds. The hero drops to a single column with the headline at 40px. The
services grid goes one-up, the work carousel keeps one card in view, and the
sticky call bar sits above the safe area. Every target clears 44px.

## What is deliberately absent

No licence numbers, certifications, awards, brand partnerships, response-time
promises or percentage claims. The business is not a licensed contractor and the
footer says so plainly. Everything stated is a fact the owner can stand behind.
